Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

IDK My Groceries is a personal shopping and budgeting tool built and operated by Logic Nerds. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information beyond what is required for the app to function. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and profile photo solely to identify your account and display your avatar. This information is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is not stored, sold, or shared by Logic Nerds.

App Data

Shopping lists, budgets, and item data you enter into the app are stored in Google Firebase under your account. This data is used only to power the app's features and is not accessed, analyzed, or shared by Logic Nerds for any other purpose.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

IDK My Groceries uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies: Google Firebase (authentication and data storage) and Google AdSense (advertising). We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach Logic Nerds at idk@logicnerds.com.
