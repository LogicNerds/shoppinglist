Start in MyBudget

No account, no sign-up. Your numbers stay in your browser until you hit Submit.

How it works

  1. Fill in MyBudget

    Your take-home pay and where it goes. Ballpark figures are fine.

  2. Hit “Submit my budget for review”

    It's under your results. Pick private or anonymous.

  3. Check your inbox

    Within 7 days you get our notes: what's working, what we'd change, and where we'd start.

Two ways to send it

Private

We email our review to you. Your name and email stay private.

Anonymous

Pick a made-up name, and we may break down your budget in a video. You don't give an email, so you won't get a written reply.

What we look at

Whether every dollar is assigned, how your money splits between cost of living, debt, your future, and lifestyle, and what we'd tackle first.

We're not licensed financial advisors, and this isn't investment, tax, or legal advice. We'll never ask for account numbers or logins. See how submissions are handled in the Privacy Policy.
