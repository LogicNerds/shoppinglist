Why is it free?

Everybody deserves access to financial knowledge, and IDK was founded with a mission to deliver that access to as many people as we can.

This is for you if

  • You get paid and it's gone, and you're not sure where it went.
  • Your debt balances aren't really going down.
  • You've tried budgeting before and it didn't stick.
  • You have less than three months of expenses saved.

What we won't do

  • Sell you anything. No course, no investments, no insurance.
  • Ask for bank logins, account numbers, or your Social Security number.
  • Lecture you about lattes. Nobody's judging your spending.
  • Give investment or tax advice. This is about your monthly cash flow.

What happens in the session

  1. Your goals

    What you want your money to do. Pay off a card, build a cushion, stop dreading the end of the month. Big or small, it counts.

  2. Your income

    What actually lands in your account each month, paycheck by paycheck.

  3. Your bills and spending

    Rent, car, debt payments, groceries, subscriptions, the stuff that sneaks up on you. Ballpark figures are fine. Having your banking app handy helps you remember what's going out, but you never have to show it to us.

  4. Assign every dollar

    We build your zero-based budget together until every dollar of income has a place to go, with your goals included.

  5. Your sheet

    You leave with your own budget sheet to keep using month after month. You won't need us to run it.
