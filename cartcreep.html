THE RULES
🎯 THE GOAL
Stay under your target budget and cash out for as many rounds as you can survive.

🛒 TOSS IN
Adds the oldest item on the belt to your cart. Its price is revealed instantly. If it pushes you over budget — game over.

✋ PASS
Skips the oldest item. Costs you -2 seconds as a penalty.

😬 MISSED ITEM
If an item slides off the belt before you decide, it's gone and costs you -3 seconds.

💳 CHECKOUT
Once your cart hits 90% of your budget, the CHECKOUT button appears. Hit it to cash out and survive to the next round.

💰 ROLLOVER
Whatever budget you have left when you checkout carries into the next round's budget.

⚡ WATCH OUT
The belt gets faster and more crowded as time ticks down — and each new round starts faster than the last.
